How We Test and Verify

Xenors separates direct testing from research. A page should not imply that a tool, model or workflow was tested unless the test was actually performed.

Technical content

For code and setup guides, verification can include running the code, checking outputs, reproducing errors and comparing behavior with current official documentation.

AI content

AI capability claims should identify the model or product being discussed and avoid turning a benchmark, demo or vendor claim into a universal guarantee.

Industrial safety

Industrial-safety articles are educational. Real installations require the machine documentation, electrical interface, risk assessment and applicable site rules to be checked before implementation.

Finance

Examples are educational and do not guarantee profit. Market prices, regulations, fees and tax treatment can change and should be verified before acting.

Updates

When a page is materially changed, its structured data and sitemap last-modified date should reflect the actual source revision date where available.
