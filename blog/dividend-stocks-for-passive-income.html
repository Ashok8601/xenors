Dividend Stocks for Passive Income

Dividend stocks provide investors with regular cash payouts while offering the potential for long-term capital appreciation. They are widely used by long-term investors seeking passive income and portfolio stability.

This guide explains how dividend stocks work, how dividends are paid, their benefits, potential risks, and strategies for building a long-term dividend portfolio.
