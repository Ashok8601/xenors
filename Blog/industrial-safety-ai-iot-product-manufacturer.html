2. Forklift Blue Spot Lights

Blue Spot Lights project a bright blue beam several meters ahead or behind a moving forklift. The projected light acts as an early visual warning, especially in areas where visibility is restricted by racks, machinery, or blind intersections.

Key Benefits:

  • Improves pedestrian awareness
  • Increases visibility at intersections
  • Suitable for warehouses
  • Helps reduce unexpected collisions
  • High-brightness LED technology
  • Industrial-grade construction

Applications:

  • Automobile Plants
  • Distribution Centers
  • Logistics Hubs
  • Manufacturing Units
  • Warehouse Operations

3. Forklift Red Zone Lights

Red Zone Lights project a clearly visible red safety line on the floor around the forklift, defining a hazardous operating area.

Workers can instantly recognize the safe distance they should maintain from moving equipment.

Advantages:

  • Improves workplace discipline
  • Reduces pedestrian intrusion
  • Supports industrial safety practices
  • Easy installation on forklifts
