9. Forklift Safety Solutions

Forklifts are among the primary causes of serious workplace accidents in warehouses and manufacturing facilities. Hesham Industrial Solutions provides end-to-end forklift safety retrofits that significantly reduce blind-spot hazards. These solutions combine visual, auditory, and electronic warnings to ensure both pedestrian workers and lift truck operators maintain situational awareness.

10. Forklift Anti Collision Systems

Hesham’s Anti Collision Systems utilize advanced ranging technologies—such as Ultra-Wideband (UWB), radar, and ultrasonic sensors—to detect approaching pedestrians or other vehicles in real time.

  • Vehicle-to-Pedestrian Detection: Warns operators and workers when distance falls below critical thresholds.
  • Automatic Speed Reduction: Integrates with vehicle controls to automatically slow down the forklift in high-risk zones.
  • 360-Degree Perimeter Coverage: Eliminates blind spots around large material handlers and reach trucks.

11. Forklift Warning Lights

Visual safety signaling is essential in noisy factory environments where acoustic alarms may be drowned out.

  • Blue Spot Light: Projects a bright blue spot on the floor 3 to 5 meters ahead or behind the forklift to warn oncoming pedestrians.
  • Red Zone Boundary Light: Casts a bright red perimeter line on both sides of the forklift to establish a strict keep-out zone for workers.
  • Arc Danger Lights: Projects a curved perimeter warning zone around the rear swing of heavy forklifts.

12. Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS)

Human error and fatigue are leading contributors to material handling accidents. The Driver Monitoring System (DMS) from Hesham Industrial Solutions utilizes dynamic AI vision to monitor driver behavior continuously.

  • Fatigue Detection: Triggers immediate alarms if yawning or eye closure is detected.
  • Distraction Warnings: Detects cell phone usage, smoking, or looking away from the aisle.
  • Seatbelt & Identity Verification: Ensures only authorized drivers operate machinery safely.

34. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What types of forklift safety lights does Hesham Industrial Solutions manufacture?

Hesham manufactures Red Zone border lights, Blue Spot/Arrow lights, Arc safety lights, dynamic laser line projectors, and flashing LED strobe beacons suitable for all electric and IC engine forklifts.

Q2. How does a Forklift Anti-Collision System work?

The system uses ultrasonic, radar, or UWB sensors to measure real-time distance between vehicles and pedestrians or obstacles. When a potential hazard enters the danger zone, visual and audio alerts are triggered, and speed limiting can automatically engage.

Q3. Are Hesham fuel transfer pumps compatible with gasoline?

Standard fuel pumps are specifically engineered for diesel, kerosene, and light oils. Specialized explosion-proof models must be selected for volatile fuels like gasoline.

Q4. Can virtual walkway projectors replace traditional paint floor marking?

Yes, virtual LED floor projectors project bright, indestructible lines and signs that never fade or wear off under heavy forklift traffic, eliminating repainting costs.
