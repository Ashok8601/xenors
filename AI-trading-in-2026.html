What is AI Trading?

AI trading combines artificial intelligence, machine learning, and market analysis to assist with investment decisions and automate parts of the trading process. While AI can analyze large amounts of data quickly, it should be used alongside sound risk management and human judgment rather than as a guaranteed way to make profits. 1
