Editorial Policy

Xenors publishes educational material about AI, technology, industrial safety, coding and finance. The goal is to explain topics clearly without presenting speculation as fact.

Source preference

When a claim depends on a product, standard, regulation or fast-changing feature, writers should prefer primary documentation and identify the date checked.

Testing and examples

Code, workflows and product instructions should distinguish between steps actually tested by Xenors and examples that readers still need to verify in their own environment.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected in the article. Readers can report an issue through the contact page with the affected URL and supporting source.

Finance

Finance and trading articles are educational, not personalized investment advice. Xenors does not promise daily income, returns or guaranteed outcomes.

Authorship

Articles identify Ashok Kumar Yadav or the Xenors editorial workflow when that attribution is present in the source material. Author information is available on the author page.
