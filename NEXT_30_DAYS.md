## Every week — internal linking and refresh work

- Update 3–5 existing pages before creating more content.
- Add at least three contextual links among pages in the same pillar.
- Check the top-impression low-CTR pages in GSC and improve title/description only when the live query intent supports the change.
- Do not create more generic “doctype html”, “robots html”, or “canonical tag” pages just to chase impressions against MDN/W3Schools-type intent.
- Inspect GSC indexing and canonical status after major URL/redirect changes.
- Review GA4 for engaged sessions, scroll depth and source/country quality rather than raw active-user spikes.

## End-of-month review

Compare the 30-day period with the prior 30 days for:

- Organic clicks and impressions by the three pillars.
- CTR for queries/pages already ranking in positions 1–15.
- Number of impressions in positions 1–3, 4–10, 11–20 and 50+.
- Engagement rate / average engagement time for organic traffic only.
- Indexing/canonical errors in Search Console.

Do not use a site-wide 30% CTR as a pass/fail target. Treat CTR together with position and query intent; the immediate objective is to move more qualified queries into the top 10 and improve snippets on pages already receiving impressions.
