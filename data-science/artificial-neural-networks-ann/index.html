Understand the computation behind Introduction to Neural Networks

A small network for tabular classification might use two dense hidden layers with ReLU activations and a sigmoid output for binary probability.

  • A neuron computes a weighted sum, adds a bias and applies an activation function.
  • Hidden layers compose many simple transformations into nonlinear functions that can approximate complex relationships.
  • Backpropagation applies the chain rule to compute gradients efficiently from the loss back through each layer.
  • Initialization, activation choice and learning rate all affect whether gradients remain useful during training.

Example 3 · Define a tiny neural baseline
# Neural Networks · example 3
import tensorflow as tf
model = tf.keras.Sequential([
    tf.keras.layers.Input(shape=(8,)),
    tf.keras.layers.Dense(16, activation='relu'),
    tf.keras.layers.Dense(1, activation='sigmoid')
])
model.compile(optimizer='adam', loss='binary_crossentropy', metrics=['accuracy'])
model.summary()
