Define what a reviewer should learn from the project

Strong starter projects include house-price regression, customer churn classification, sales forecasting, recommendation baselines, sentiment analysis, fraud-risk exploration, delivery-time prediction, employee attrition analysis, image classification and dashboard-based KPI analysis.

  • A beginner project should be small enough to finish but rich enough to show problem framing, cleaning, analysis and communication.
  • Projects are more convincing when they compare a baseline with an improved approach and explain why the improvement matters.
  • A README should state the question, dataset, method, metric, limitations and exact steps needed to reproduce the result.
  • Choose public datasets with clear licenses and avoid projects that depend on inaccessible private data.
