Understand the role of the field in the data-science workflow

Imagine a retail team trying to reduce stockouts. A useful data science workflow would collect transaction history, clean missing product records, explore seasonality, build a demand baseline, test a forecasting model and measure whether the recommendations actually reduce missed sales.

  • In real analysis, the field is valuable because it makes one part of the pipeline explicit.
  • Data science combines programming, statistics, domain knowledge and experimentation to turn raw data into decisions.
  • Record the choice you made, why you made it and what evidence would make you change it.
  • A practical learning order starts with data handling and Python, then SQL, statistics, visualization, machine learning and finally deployment-oriented projects.
