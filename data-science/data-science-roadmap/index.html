Understand the role of the learning path in the data-science workflow

A realistic six-month plan might spend the first month on Python and SQL, the second on statistics and EDA, months three and four on machine learning, month five on two end-to-end projects, and month six on interview practice plus a specialization.

  • A sensible learning path moves from Python and SQL into statistics, exploratory analysis, machine learning and finally portfolio projects.
  • Do not wait to finish every theory topic before building; alternate short study blocks with small projects so concepts become usable skills.
  • SQL and data cleaning are often more important in entry-level work than advanced neural networks because most real datasets are messy and relational.
  • A portfolio is stronger when each project explains the question, data quality, baseline, metric, error analysis and final limitation.
