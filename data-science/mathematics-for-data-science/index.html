Understand the role of Math & Statistics for Data Science in the data-science workflow

A churn model can be explained mathematically as a sequence: standardize numerical variables, multiply feature values by learned weights, transform the score into a probability, then compare predictions with observed outcomes.

  • The useful part of the topic is not the label but the behavior it explains.
  • Statistics helps summarize data and reason about uncertainty; probability gives a language for uncertain outcomes; linear algebra represents data compactly; calculus explains how optimization changes model parameters.
  • A good check is to reproduce the idea on a tiny dataset before scaling it to a notebook or dashboard.
  • You do not need to become a pure mathematician before building models.

# Math & Statistics for Data Science · example 1
import numpy as np
x = np.array([2.0, 4.0, 6.0])
print('mean =', x.mean())
print('variance =', x.var(ddof=1))
print('norm =', np.linalg.norm(x))
