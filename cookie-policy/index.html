Cookie Policy

This policy explains browser storage and optional third-party services used on Xenors.

Consent first

Optional analytics and subscription services are not initialized until you make a consent choice. Your choice is stored in localStorage so the site can remember it.

Google Analytics 4

If you accept analytics, Xenors may load Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate page usage, navigation and engagement. Consent Mode v2 keeps analytics storage denied until you accept.

Web push

If configured by the site owner, Xenors can use OneSignal for browser push notifications. Push permission is requested only after consent and an explicit click on the follow button.

Email subscriptions

If configured by the site owner, the email form sends the address you enter to the hosted newsletter provider shown by the form. The provider is responsible for its own retention and unsubscribe controls.

Your controls

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer to reopen the consent panel. You can also clear site storage or notification permissions in your browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Xenors contact page.
