What Is a Pedestrian Detection AI System?

A pedestrian detection AI system is a computer-vision safety solution that analyzes live video and identifies people within monitored areas.

Traditional CCTV simply displays or records video. A human detection camera combined with AI software actively evaluates the scene and determines whether a person is present within a configured detection area.

A typical industrial workflow is:

Camera → AI Processing → Human Detection → Safety-Zone Validation → Event Confirmation → PLC / Alarm Output → Evidence Recording

This distinction is important.

A normal camera asks an operator to notice danger.

An AI camera continuously watches the scene and can generate an automated detection event when defined conditions are met.

For industrial applications, this means the camera and software can monitor areas such as:

  • robotic welding cells
  • assembly lines
  • material-handling zones
  • automated production lines
  • machine loading areas
  • restricted zones
  • robot work cells
  • warehouse intersections
  • transfer lines
  • dangerous machine-access areas

The objective is not simply to detect movement. It is to identify a human and then determine whether that human is located inside an area where detection matters.

How a Pedestrian Detection AI Camera Works

A pedestrian detection AI camera system receives a live video stream from one or more industrial or IP cameras.

Each video frame is passed to an artificial-intelligence model trained to recognize the human form.

When a person is detected, the system calculates the detection position relative to the configured area.

For industrial floor zones, HESHAM’s software can evaluate the bottom-center or feet position of the detected person. This is useful because the person’s feet generally correspond more closely to their actual location on the factory floor than the center of the entire bounding box.

The logic then becomes:

  1. Capture live camera frame.
  2. Run human-detection inference.
  3. Identify a person.
  4. Determine the person’s floor position.
  5. Check whether that position is inside the active ROI.
  6. Check whether it falls inside an ignore zone.
  7. Evaluate Dynamic ROI state.
  8. Apply confirmation timing.
  9. Generate a confirmed event.
  10. Activate the configured output or PLC signal.
  11. Save evidence and event information.

This makes the system significantly more useful than a basic motion detector.

Movement from machinery, shadows, welding equipment or other objects does not automatically have to be treated as a pedestrian event.

AI Safety Camera vs CCTV Safety Camera vs Certified Safety Devices

The phrase safety camera covers very different things, so it helps to separate them before choosing a solution.

TypeWhat it doesTypical role
CCTV safety cameraShows and records video. A person has to watch it.General visibility and incident review

Safety light curtain, safety laser scanner or certified safety vision systemPurpose-built safety devices evaluated against machine-safety standards.Safety functions that must stop or limit a machine

Standards such as ISO 13849-1 and IEC 62061 describe how safety-related control functions are designed, and IEC 61496 covers electro-sensitive protective equipment such as light curtains and laser scanners. An AI camera that is not certified for a safety function should not be the only thing that stops a hazardous machine.

The practical approach is to use the AI camera for what it does well: recognizing people, adding zone logic, giving the PLC an extra signal and keeping evidence. The risk assessment for the machine then decides which certified devices are also required.

Main ROI: Detect People Only Where It Matters

One of the most important parts of a good pedestrian detection AI system is the ability to define exactly where a detection should count.

Suppose a camera sees both:

  • a robotic welding cell, and
  • a safe pedestrian walkway outside the cell.

Without ROI logic, every visible person could create an unnecessary alarm.

HESHAM’s software allows a Main ROI polygon to be configured independently for each camera.

Human detections outside this polygon can be excluded from the safety decision.

This makes the AI camera more practical for real industrial scenes.

AI Ignore Zones

Even inside the main detection area, some regions may need to be excluded.

For example:

  • reflections through windows
  • operator screens
  • safe platforms
  • neighboring walkways
  • inaccessible sections
  • visual areas that should not participate in the safety decision

The system therefore supports AI Ignore Zones.

The supplied configuration supports up to five ignore polygons per camera.

This provides much finer control than simply drawing one large rectangular detection zone.

PLC-Controlled Dynamic ROI

Industrial areas are not always static.

A zone that is dangerous while a robot is operating may be safe during maintenance.

Another zone may need monitoring only when a fixture, conveyor or machine is in a particular state.

This is where Dynamic ROI becomes useful.

A Dynamic ROI polygon can be activated or deactivated according to a PLC signal.

For example:

Robot Running = Dynamic ROI ON

When the relevant PLC bit becomes active, that zone participates in human-detection logic.

When the condition is inactive, the same zone can remain reserved but excluded from active detection.

The supplied five-camera configuration supports up to 10 globally numbered Dynamic ROI polygons.

This creates a direct connection between machine state and AI-camera monitoring.

What Affects Human Detection AI Camera Accuracy

No vendor can promise the same result in every plant. These site factors have the biggest effect on a human detection AI camera:

  • Camera position and angle: a view that shows the person's full body and feet works better than a sharply cropped or overhead-only view.
  • Lighting: glare, deep shadow and changing light change what the camera records.
  • Occlusion: machinery, fixtures and stacked material can hide part of a person.
  • Welding light and sparks: bright flashes can disturb the image in welding cells.
  • Resolution and distance: people far from the camera take up fewer pixels.
  • Stream stability: dropped frames or network faults reduce reliability, which is why camera health monitoring matters.

Before commissioning, run the system on footage from your own cell, and record both missed detections and false alarms. Adjust camera placement, ROI, ignore zones and the confirmation timer based on what you see.

AI Camera vs RFID Pedestrian Detection

Another common question is whether factories should use AI cameras or RFID/tag-based systems.

They solve similar problems differently.

AI Camera

Detects visible human form using computer vision.

Advantages can include:

  • no tag required for each person
  • detects visitors and contractors when visible
  • visual evidence
  • camera view available
  • event snapshots/video
  • configurable image-based zones

RFID / UWB

Detects a compatible tag using radio technology.

Advantages can include:

  • precise proximity behavior in suitable architectures
  • does not depend on visual appearance in the same way
  • useful where controlled tag compliance is practical

The correct choice depends on site layout, risk assessment and operating conditions.

Some industrial safety architectures may even combine technologies rather than treating them as competitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pedestrian detection AI camera?

A pedestrian detection AI camera is a camera whose video is analyzed by artificial-intelligence software to recognize people. Unlike a standard CCTV camera, the analysis can raise an event when a person enters a configured zone, and that event can be logged or sent to a PLC or alarm.

What is a pedestrian detection AI system?

A pedestrian detection AI system is the complete setup around the camera: one or more cameras, a GPU-equipped computer, human detection software, zone (ROI) logic, event recording and a PLC or alarm interface. The camera supplies video; the system decides what counts as a confirmed detection and what signal to send.

What is a human detection AI camera?

A human detection AI camera is an AI camera set up to detect people specifically, rather than any movement. In this system the detection is run on the camera stream by software, so detections can be limited to chosen zones and filtered with a confirmation timer.

What is a safety camera, and how is an AI safety camera different?

A safety camera in the everyday sense is a CCTV camera used to watch an area. An AI safety camera adds software that recognizes people and raises events. Neither is automatically a certified machine-safety device; certified safety functions normally use purpose-built devices such as safety light curtains, safety laser scanners and safety PLCs.

Can an AI camera detect humans in real time?

Yes, a properly configured AI vision system can process camera streams continuously and detect people in near real time. Actual latency depends on the model, GPU, camera stream, resolution and configuration, so it should be validated on your own installation.

Does it support PLC input and output signals?

Yes. PLC inputs can control conditions such as Dynamic ROI states, while outputs can communicate Human Detected, System OK, System Fault and a software heartbeat.

Can the software save detection snapshots and event video?

Yes. Confirmed events can create a snapshot with event information, and the recording configuration can retain pre-event and post-event footage, for example about 10 seconds before and after a detection.

Does the system need an internet connection?

Can it detect people only inside a specific area?

Yes. Main ROI polygons limit detection decisions to selected areas, ignore zones can exclude parts of them, and Dynamic ROI zones can be switched on or off by PLC signals.

Is an RTX 3060 enough for human detection?

An RTX 3060 is a practical GPU for a multi-camera industrial human-detection configuration when camera count, resolution, frame rate and AI model are properly engineered. Final performance must be validated on the exact deployment.

How accurate is a pedestrian detection AI system?

There is no single accuracy figure that applies to every site. Results depend on camera angle and height, lighting, occlusion by machinery, resolution and the zone setup. Test the system on footage from your own cell and record missed detections and false alarms before commissioning.

Does AI pedestrian detection replace machine guarding or safety devices?

No. AI vision should be added as an extra layer inside an approved machine-safety design. Certified guarding, emergency stops, safety PLCs, interlocks and other legally required safety systems remain necessary where applicable.
