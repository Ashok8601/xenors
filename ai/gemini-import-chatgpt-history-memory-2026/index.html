Gemini Can Import Your ChatGPT History & Memory — Here’s How It Works

Google now lets eligible Gemini users bring over memory, preferences and full chat history from other AI assistants. That means switching from ChatGPT or Claude no longer has to mean starting from zero. The feature is useful—but the privacy, account and regional limits matter before you upload years of conversations.

Quick answer: Gemini can import a ChatGPT data-export ZIP and can also import a summary of your preferences and remembered context. Google currently documents a 5 GB ZIP limit, up to five ZIP uploads per day, and support for eligible adults using personal Google Accounts. Work, school and supervised accounts are excluded. Availability also varies by region.

Google just removed one of the biggest reasons people avoid switching AI assistants

For years, changing AI assistants created a hidden switching cost: your new tool did not know your preferences, projects or previous discussions. You could move manually, but rebuilding context was tedious.

Google's latest Gemini import workflow changes that. Gemini can now accept two different kinds of context from another AI assistant: a memory-style summary of what the other assistant knows about you, and a ZIP archive of full chat history. Google's own instructions explicitly describe ChatGPT and Claude export flows. citeturn515038search0turn515038search2

This is more important than a normal settings update. AI assistants are becoming long-lived workspaces. If users can move their history between platforms, the competitive battleground shifts from “who already knows me?” toward model quality, tools, privacy, pricing and integrations.

How to import ChatGPT memory and preferences into Gemini

Gemini's memory import does not require downloading your entire chat archive. Google gives you a prompt that you copy into the old assistant. That assistant generates a summary of your preferences and contextual information, and you paste that response back into Gemini. citeturn515038search0turn515038search2

  1. Open Gemini with an eligible personal Google Account.
  2. Open Settings → Import memory to Gemini.
  3. Copy Gemini's provided memory-export prompt.
  4. Paste that prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or another supported AI assistant.
  5. Copy the generated summary.
  6. Return to Gemini, paste the summary and add it to memory.
Best practice: read the generated memory summary before importing it. Remove secrets, outdated facts, sensitive work information or anything you would not want carried into another AI platform.

If you mainly want Gemini to understand your writing style, interests, ongoing projects and preferences, this lighter memory-transfer route may be more sensible than uploading years of conversations.

How to move your full ChatGPT chat history to Gemini

Google also supports a file-based import for full conversation history. For ChatGPT, Google instructs users to export account data first, then upload the resulting ZIP archive into Gemini's import settings. citeturn515038search2

  1. In ChatGPT, open your account settings and use the data-export option.
  2. Download the ZIP archive when the export is ready.
  3. Open Gemini on the web and go to Settings → Import memory to Gemini.
  4. Under Import chats, choose Add.
  5. Select the exported ZIP file.
  6. Wait for processing to finish. Google notes that large imports can take time.

After import, conversations can appear in Gemini's chat history with an imported-chat indicator, and you can continue from those threads instead of recreating context manually. citeturn515038search2

Do not upload a raw history archive blindly. A long-running ChatGPT account may contain personal data, customer information, credentials pasted by mistake, private business discussions or old assumptions that are no longer accurate. Review your risk before importing.

The privacy question matters more than the convenience

The most important part of this feature is not the upload button—it is what happens after your data enters a second AI ecosystem.

Google states that imported and continued chats are saved in Gemini Activity and handled under Google's privacy framework. Google also recently introduced more granular controls around whether Gemini chat history is used for generative-AI training, while still retaining some data for service operation and safety depending on settings. citeturn515038search1turn515038search2

Before importing, check:

  • whether the export contains business-confidential or regulated data;
  • whether you actually need full history or only a memory summary;
  • your Gemini activity and model-training settings;
  • whether old conversations contain passwords, API keys or private identifiers;
  • whether your employer allows consumer AI tools for work data.

For most people, the safer first move is to import a cleaned memory summary, test Gemini for a week, and only then decide whether full history is worth transferring.

Should you switch from ChatGPT to Gemini now?

This feature makes switching easier, but it does not make the choice automatic. ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude increasingly overlap in writing, research, coding, files and agent-style workflows. The right choice depends on which ecosystem fits your actual work.

Why this could become one of the most important AI-platform features of 2026

Model launches get attention, but switching costs shape markets. Once users accumulate hundreds or thousands of conversations, personalization becomes a moat. Import tools weaken that moat.

That creates three likely effects:

  1. More AI-tool experimentation: users can test alternatives without losing years of context.
  2. More pressure on privacy controls: portability means more copies of the same personal history can exist across providers.
  3. More competition on actual product quality: tools must win on reasoning, speed, integrations, agents and trust rather than simply owning historical context.

It also means “AI memory portability” is becoming a real product category. Expect more users to search for ways to move ChatGPT history, migrate Claude conversations, transfer AI memory and compare what each platform does with imported context.

FAQ

Can Gemini import ChatGPT conversations?

Yes. Google's current help documentation describes importing a ZIP export of ChatGPT history into Gemini for eligible users.

Can Gemini import ChatGPT memory?

Yes. Gemini provides a prompt-based memory transfer workflow that turns your preferences and contextual information into a summary you can paste into Gemini.

What is the maximum Gemini import size?

Google currently documents a 5 GB maximum per ZIP file, with up to five ZIP uploads per day.

Can I import chats with a work or school Google account?

Not currently. Google's help page says the feature is for eligible adults using personal Google Accounts.

Is Gemini import available in India?

Google's current exclusion list names the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Availability can still vary by rollout and product surface, so check your Gemini settings directly.

Will Gemini use imported chats for training?

Imported chats are handled under Gemini activity and privacy controls. Review Google's current training and activity settings before importing sensitive data.
