Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What kind of Tech support does Xenors provide?

We specialize in Python Frameworks like Flask and Django. If you are building an API or a full-stack application and facing issues with authentication or database persistence, our team can provide guidance through our detailed technical blogs and direct consultations.

2. Can I request a tutorial on a specific AI tool or ML algorithm?

Yes! We love exploring new Machine Learning models. Whether you want to learn about Regression, Classification, or advanced Neural Networks, just send us a message. We prioritize content that helps our users solve real-world problems.

3. How do you analyze Stock Market data?

At Xenors, we use historical OHLC data and Python libraries like Pandas and NumPy to create prediction models. While we provide technical analysis, we always encourage our users to combine AI insights with their own research for financial growth.

4. Are the resources on Xenors.in free for students?

Absolutely. Our mission is to provide high-quality digital literacy and coding tutorials for free. We believe that professional communication and technical skills should be accessible to every Computer Science Engineering student, especially those from regional areas like Sonbhadra, UP.

5. Do you offer guest blogging opportunities?

We are always open to collaborating with fellow content creators. If you have deep insights into Gadgets, Finance, or AI news, we would love to feature your expertise on Xenors.in to help our international audience in the USA, UK, and Australia.

How to Contact Xenors

The Xenors contact page is the right place for editorial corrections, technical questions about published tutorials, partnership enquiries, accessibility feedback and general questions about the website. A clear message helps us understand the request and route it to the right type of follow-up.

Report an error or outdated article

If you find a factual error, broken link, outdated software instruction or an important source that no longer supports a statement, include the page URL and the exact section that needs attention. For technical issues, mention the software version, operating system or environment when relevant. For finance pages, include the market, date or source that shows why a statement may need review.

Suggest a topic

Topic suggestions are welcome when they fit the site's main areas: artificial intelligence, machine learning, coding, backend development, data, technology, investing and personal-finance education. The most useful suggestions describe the question a reader is trying to answer. A precise problem is often more valuable than a broad request to “write about AI” or “cover stocks.”

Business and partnership enquiries

For partnerships, describe the organization, the proposed collaboration and what value it would provide to Xenors readers. Commercial relationships should not require misleading claims, hidden endorsements or editorial control that prevents honest discussion of limitations. If a relationship could affect how readers interpret a page, appropriate disclosure is important.

Guest contributions

If you want to contribute an article, send a short pitch before sending a full draft. Explain the topic, intended audience, why the article adds something that is not already covered on Xenors, and your relevant experience. Original work is required. Material copied, lightly paraphrased from another site or created only to place promotional links is not a good fit.

Technical support expectations

Xenors publishes educational tutorials, but the contact page is not a guaranteed one-to-one support service for every external product or broker mentioned in an article. For account access, billing, exchange execution, regulated financial services or vendor-specific outages, contact the official provider. We can improve our explanatory content when a tutorial is unclear, but we cannot act as the provider's customer support team.

Financial-content questions

Finance articles on Xenors are educational. Please do not send confidential financial records, account passwords, card details, brokerage credentials or identity documents. We cannot provide personalized investment, tax or legal advice through a general website contact form. For decisions that depend on your personal circumstances, consider an appropriately qualified professional in your jurisdiction.

Privacy and security

Only share the information reasonably necessary for your enquiry. Never send passwords, API secrets, private keys or one-time authentication codes. If your message concerns a potential security issue on Xenors, explain the issue carefully without accessing data that does not belong to you or disrupting the service. Responsible reports are easier to investigate when they include steps to reproduce the problem and the affected URL.

Accessibility feedback

We want Xenors to remain usable with different devices, zoom levels, keyboards and assistive technologies. If a page has unreadable contrast, inaccessible navigation, missing alternative text, a form that cannot be completed by keyboard or another barrier, tell us which page and device or browser you were using. Accessibility feedback can directly influence site improvements.

What to include in your message

  • The URL or section you are referring to.
  • A concise subject describing the issue or proposal.
  • Enough context to reproduce a technical problem.
  • Links to public evidence when suggesting a factual correction.
  • Your preferred method for a reply, if the contact form supports it.
